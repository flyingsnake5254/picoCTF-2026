$a \equiv b\,(mod\,n)$
代表 $(a-b)\,可以被\, n \,整除$

### <span style="color: #42a5f5; font-weight: bold;">四則運算</span>

- **加、減法：**
$$
\begin{aligned}
(a+b)\,mod\,n=[(a\,mod\,n)\,+\,(b\,mod\,n)]\,mod\,n\\
(a-b)\,mod\,n=[(a\,mod\,n)\,-\,(b\,mod\,n)]\,mod\,n
\end{aligned}
$$

- **乘法：**
$$
(a\times b)\,\,mod\,\,n=[(a\,\,mod\,\,n)(b\,\,mod\,\,n)\,\,\,mod\,\,n]
$$

- <span style="color: #ffee00; font-weight: bold;">次方</span>
$$
a^k\,\,mod\,\,n=[(a\,\,mod\,\,n)^k]\,\,mod\,\,n
$$

- **等價替換**
$$
\begin{aligned}
if\,\,\,\,\,\,a\equiv b\,\,\,(mod\,\,n)\\
then\,\,\,\,\,a^k\equiv b^k\,\,\,(mod\,\,n)
\end{aligned}
$$

- **RSA 核心關係**
$$
M^{ed} \equiv M\,\,\,(mod\,\,n)
$$

### <span style="color: #42a5f5; font-weight: bold;">快速冪算法</span>

計算：
$$7^{13}\,\,\,\,mod\,\,\,33$$
- **Step 1 : 把指數轉成二進位**
  $$13_{10} = 1101_{2}$$
  也就是
$$13=2^3+2^2+2^0=8+4+1$$
- **Step 2 : 預先計算平方序列**
  從 $7^1$ 開始，不斷平方（每次都 mod）
$$
\begin{aligned}  
7^1 \,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33 & = 7\\  
7^2 \,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33 & = 16\\  
7^4 \,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33 & = (7^2)^2 \,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33 \\  
& = (16 \times 16) \,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33 \\  
& = 25\\  
7^8 \,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33 & = (7^4)^2 \,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33 \\  
& = (25 \times 25) \,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33 \\  
& = 31  
\end{aligned}
$$
- **Step 3 : 根據二進位挑選剛剛運算結果**
$$
\begin{aligned}  
7^{13} &\,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33\\  
&= (7^8 \cdot 7^4 \cdot 7^1)\,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33\\  
&= (31 \times 25 \times 7)\,\,\, \text{mod} \,\,\, 33\\  
&= 13  
\end{aligned}
$$

### <span style="color: #42a5f5; font-weight: bold;">歐幾里得算法</span>

用來求兩個整數的最大公因數
$$\color{yellow}gcd(a,b)=gcd(b,a\,\,\,mod\,\,\,b)$$

**例子**
$$
\begin{aligned}
gcd(48,18)&=gcd(18,\,\,\,48\,\,\,mod\,\,\,18)\\
&=gcd(18,\,\,\,12)\\
&=gcd(12,\,\,\,18\,\,\,mod\,\,\,12)\\
&=gcd(12,\,\,\,6)\\
&=gcd(6,\,\,\,0) \\
\therefore gcd=6
\end{aligned}
$$
或寫成：
$$
\begin{aligned}
gcd(48,18)\\
48&=18 \times 2 + 12\\
18&=12 \times 1+6\\
12&=6 \times 2+0\\
\therefore gcd=6
\end{aligned}
$$

### <span style="color: #42a5f5; font-weight: bold;">擴展歐幾里得算法</span>

用來求
$$\color{yellow} ax+by=gcd(a,b)$$

**例子**
$$240x+46y=gcd(240,46)\tag{求x,y}$$
- **Step 1 : 先做一般 gcd**
$$
\begin{align}
求\,\,\,gcd(240,46):\\
240&=46 \times 5+10\tag{①}\\
46&=10 \times 4 +6\tag{②}\\
10&=6 \times1+4\tag{③}\\
6&=4\times1+2\tag{④}\\
4&=2\times2+0\tag{⑤}\\
\therefore gcd=2
\end{align}
$$

- **Step 2 : 往回代**

$$
\begin{align}
2&=6-4\tag{根據\,\,④}\\
&=6-(10-6)\tag{根據\,\,③，把 4 替換掉}\\
&=6-10+6=2\times6-10\tag{整理}\\
&=2(46-10\times4)-10\tag{根據\,\,②，把\,6\,替換掉}\\
&=2\times46-8\times10-10=2\times46-9\times10\tag{整理}\\
&=2\times46-9(240-46\times5)\tag{根據\,\,①，把\,10\,替換掉}\\
&=2\times46-9\times240+45\times46\tag{整理}\\
&=-9\times240+47\times46\\
\therefore x=-9，y=47
\end{align}
$$

### <span style="color: #42a5f5; font-weight: bold;">模反元素</span>

給定整數 $a$ 和模數 $m$ ：
$$
a\,\,\,mod\,\,\,m=k
$$
若存在一個整數 $x$ ，使得：
$$
(a\,\,\,\cdot \,\,\,x)\,\,\,mod\,\,\,m=1 
$$
那這個 $x$ 叫做：
$$
\color{yellow} a\,\,\,在\,\,\,mod\,\,\,m\,\,\,下的模反元素
$$
<div style="height: 1px; background-image: linear-gradient(to right, #aaa 20%, rgba(255,255,255,0) 0%);
background-size: 15px 1px; background-repeat: repeat-x; margin: 1.5em 0;"></div>

**例子**

找 $3$ 在 $mod\,\,\,7$ 下的反元素 $x$：
$$
3x\,\,\,mod\,\,\,7=1
$$
利用$\color{yellow} 擴展歐幾里得：$
$$
\begin{align}
&3x\,\,\,mod\,\,\,7=1\\\\
&等同於：\\
&3x+7y=1\\
&7=3\times2+1\\
&1=7-3\times2\\\\
&\therefore x=-2,\,\,\,y=1
\end{align}
$$
